Source Ledger

Not an answer. An evidence file.

For every analysis, LexTutor produces a source ledger: the exact article, the exact judgment, their rank in the hierarchy of norms, their status in force and any contradictions detected. Each line of the ledger can be checked against the official source in one click.

In Swiss law, the code defines the identity of the provision. Art. 368 of the Code of Obligations deals with defects in the work; Art. 368 of the Civil Code belongs to adult protection law. The ledger keeps them apart — and shows it.

Disciplined method

Prepare. Verify. Limit.

The prototype shows what a practitioner must be able to verify. It does not disclose the internal details that create the product advantage.

Prepare

Assemble a public-source file: applicable provisions, published decisions, normative rank and force status.

Verify

Draft only from the assembled file. Every proposition must carry a source that can be checked.

Limit

State what the sources do not support. LexTutor prepares the evidence; judgment remains professional.

What stays deliberately discreet

Three gestures, no public black box.

  • No internal execution detail is published.
  • No client data is used in the prototype.
  • Detailed technical documentation is shared only in qualified diligence.
